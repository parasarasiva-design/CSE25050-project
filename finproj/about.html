About Our University

30 years of academic excellence, research and values.

Our Mission

To provide world-class education accessible to all, while nurturing strong values, creativity and social responsibility.

Our Vision

To be among the top 100 universities in the world by 2030 — known for excellence, ethics, and inclusivity.

Our History

Founded in 1994 with just 200 students, today we serve over 25,000 students across 10 campuses in India.

Director's Message

— Dr. R. Krishnan, Director
